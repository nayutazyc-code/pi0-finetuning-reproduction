## Motion and progress measures

The new [offline diagnostic](../src/pi0_repro/motion_quality.py) reports per-frame maxima across the 12 measured arm joints, avoiding dilution by inactive joints. Active-motion jerk p99 and p95 are primary jump measures, alongside maximum physical joint increment. Active samples require maximum joint speed above 0.01 rad/s. A stationary trajectory has no active-motion smoothness score (`None`), rather than receiving an ideal zero score. Active duration, joint path, and all-frame extremes remain available for context.

Observed low-motion dwell requires at least 0.5 s with maximum physical arm speed below 0.015 rad/s and maximum commanded gripper speed below 0.05 normalized units/s. Frames satisfying inside-box, released, supported, and stable conditions are excluded so required success holds do not count as a pause. Phase-specific spans distinguish approach, near grasp, carrying, and inside-box observations, using recorded task flags rather than changing action selection. These are thresholded diagnostics: deliberate holds may qualify, and low movement does not uniquely identify a policy stall or its cause.

Pinch, box entry, and success coverage are reported separately; transport comparisons require comparable reached stages. An incomplete or stationary failure must not be described as smoother successful transport because it moves less. No composite rank trades missing progress invisibly against lower jerk. Derivatives use the simulation period, not a wall-clock servo guarantee. Inference underruns, application intervals, and display timing remain distinct from physical-motion measures.

Four diagnostic unit checks support definitions and edge cases, including stationary/unscored behavior and required-hold exclusion. They do not establish a model or visual-performance result. Selection must consider active jump tails, dwell spans, stage coverage, and retained failures together, while reporting completion secondarily as explicitly requested.
